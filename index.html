Thresholds were calculated independently for each raster using the tertiles (33% and 66%) of their own frequency distribution, splitting the valid pixels of each image into three classes (low, medium, and high probability) adapted to the dynamics of their respective data. Pixel aggregation when zooming out is done by averaging.
After validation, the AEF model performs better at detecting formal settlement areas, while the TESSERA model performs better at detecting informal settlement areas. We therefore extracted only the high ISP values from TESSERA and the low ISP values from AEF, which we then overlaid. The intersection of the two was considered a medium ISP. We then applied a 10×10 median filter to the result.
A flood is a rapid or slow submersion of an area that is normally dry; here, it occurs when the river overflows its minor bed and floods the surrounding areas.

Source: Mayotte Observatory, catalogue.observatoire-de-mayotte.fr
Landslides are one of the two types of ground movement considered and mapped in Mayotte. They involve the gravitational movement of a volume of loose soil on a slope, ranging from a few m³ to several hundred thousand m³. These events can be fast or slow; earthworks, the presence of water, and earthquakes can all act as triggers for these landslides.

Source: Mayotte Observatory, catalogue.observatoire-de-mayotte.fr
